## Purpose

Freeze a repository-grounded definition of what HAHAWEEK is at the current engineering state, without changing production semantics.

The definition must distinguish:
- the authoritative evidence/processing foundation;
- derived formation, validation, research, and surveillance capabilities;
- operator-facing capabilities;
- non-goals and authority boundaries.

This contract exists to prevent README, documentation, analysis, or future implementation from silently redefining HAHAWEEK.

## Canonical definition boundary

HAHAWEEK is an evidence-first blockchain research and engineering system for reconstructing early on-chain formations from preserved evidence through deterministic acquisition, canonical processing, lineage, validation, durable processing results, and auditable derived outputs.

The authoritative foundation is evidence and verified processing state, not a prediction, ranking, trading, publication, or surveillance conclusion.

## Required distinctions

1. Raw/canonical evidence is authoritative within its defined ownership boundary.
2. Canonical decision, lineage/transition/generation, durable processing-result verification, processing context, authority, and cursor retain their existing semantic owners.
3. Formation/validation/research outputs are derived and must remain traceable to evidence.
4. Surveillance is derived, evidence-linked, versioned, reproducible, and non-authoritative.
5. ADDRESS != ACTOR.
6. No predictive score, ranking, trading action, or ownership/actor conclusion may become authoritative without a separate valid contract and evidence/validation basis.
7. Operator procedures must remain repository-grounded.
8. V4 production activation remains blocked until Gate 2 PASS.

## Operator Acceptance

The definition must remain understandable to the human operator without inventing commands or procedures. It must explain what HAHAWEEK does, what it does not do, which layer is authoritative, and where the operator must STOP/FAIL-CLOSED.

## Surveillance boundary

Any definition or documentation produced under STEP 594 must preserve:
- no raw/canonical evidence mutation;
- no cursor advancement;
- no new authority;
- no automated action/trading;
- no unsupported actor/ownership inference;
- no temporal leakage;
- no unsupported scoring/risk.

Compatibility or documentation does not authorize implementation.

## Authorized scope

Any production semantic change requires a new contract.
